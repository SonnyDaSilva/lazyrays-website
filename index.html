OUR STORY

Lazyrays Games is a small indie studio based in Birmingham, UK, focusing on colourful, competitive, multiplayer party games. We release on all major consoles - Nintendo Switch, PlayStation and XBOX, and we don't plan on stopping there!
‍
We are a tight-knit team comprised of multitalented individuals that thrive in the chaotic space of game development.
‍
Lazyrays Games was founded in 2019 by one ambitious developer in his bedroom. As we grow, we strive to continue finding joy and wonder in every project.
