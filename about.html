The Lazyrays mantra

Prototype:

Making creativity the limit.

Agile development and a tight-knit team allows us to pivot quickly and streamline our concept-to-prototype pipeline.

PLAY:

Having fun, building fun.

Mechanics are tweaked, bugs are found and a clearer image of the game is established by playing our games during development.

Polish:

Breathing life into projects.

Refined animations and particle effects, small game-feel tweaks and a crucial, final bit of juice turn assets into an immersive game.

What we do

Versatile development

With an adaptable and fast team, we pivot quickly and never shy away from evolving our concepts on the go. We see every project as an opportunity to become better at what we do, tackling all ideas, no matter how hard to implement. It's these foundations that allow us to build better and more complex games as we grow.

Fun-first approach

Some want you to shake in fear, some want you to relax and unwind, but our games aim to bring out your competitive spirit and provide a fun experience for everyone! From the exhilarating high of 3-starring the hardest level to yelling at your friends for pushing you off the map, every aspect of our games is created with maximal fun in mind.

Multiplayer Experiences

We believe that games are best enjoyed together, so we focus on creating memorable moments that can be shared with family and friends. With both local and online multiplayer games, you can connect with your peers whether you prefer couch gaming sessions or long nights on Discord.

Unique Designs

Art makes or breaks a game. With most of our team consisting of creatives, we pay close attention to world building and character design. We love lively scenes and cutesy animals; our attention to detail spans everything from animations to sounds, to colour palettes.
